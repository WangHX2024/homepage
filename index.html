Hi! I am an undergraduate student in the Chu Kochen Honors College at Zhejiang University, majoring in Computer Science and Technology.

I will join the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2027, advised by Prof. Huajun Chen and Prof. Ningyu Zhang in ZJUNLP.

My research interests lie in LLM interpretability, agentic LLMs, and automated scientific research. I am particularly interested in understanding the internal mechanisms of large models and building AI agents that can reliably assist scientific discovery.
